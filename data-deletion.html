Data deletion instructions

Comments Extractor · Last updated: 1 October 2026

If you commented on a post and want your data deleted

  1. Email gookkuul@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my data".
  2. Tell us your Instagram username (we need it to work out your code, because we don't store usernames in readable form). A link to a post you commented on helps, but isn't required.
  3. We may ask you to confirm you control that Instagram account, for example by messaging us from it, so nobody can delete someone else's data by mistake.
  4. We delete every comment linked to your code from our files and confirm by email within 30 days.

If you are the Instagram account owner

  1. Remove access yourself on Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → Active → remove this app. This stops all further access straight away.
  2. Email gookkuul@gmail.com with the subject "Delete account data" from the email linked to the account (or tell us the username). We delete the posts, comments, token and analysis files for your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deletion removes the data from our working files. It can't recall anything the client already received in an aggregated report, because that report doesn't contain data traceable to you.
