Comments Extractor

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Comments Extractor is a small private tool run by Gokulakrishna Ramachandran, an independent IT consultant based in Chennai, India. It is not a public app. It is used for one client at a time, and only on Instagram accounts whose owners have personally logged in and said yes. If you commented on a post, or you're the account owner, these pages explain in plain English what happens to your data and how to get it deleted.
