Terms of use

Comments Extractor is a small private tool run by Gokulakrishna Ramachandran, an independent IT consultant based in Chennai, India. It is not a public app. It is used for one client at a time, and only on Instagram accounts whose owners have personally logged in and said yes. If you've landed here because you commented on a post, or you're the account owner, this page explains in plain English what happens to your data and how to get it deleted.

Privacy Policy

1. Who we are and what this tool does

The tool reads posts and comments from an Instagram Creator or Business account belonging to our client (Nivedita (The Women of Cinema) ), using Meta's official Instagram API ("Instagram API with Instagram Login"). The client uses the results to understand, in aggregate, how their audience responds to their content. We process this data on the client's instructions, for the client's own account, and for no other purpose.

2. What we collect

From the account owner

An access token that Instagram gives us after the owner logs in on Instagram's own page and approves access. We never see or store the owner's Instagram password.

From the account's posts

Captions, dates, post type, links, and like and comment counts.

From the comments on those posts

Comment text, date and time, comment like count, and the commenter's public Instagram username.

What we do not collect

Passwords, direct messages, follower or following lists, commenters' profile details, photos or videos of commenters, data from other accounts, or any account that has not approved access.

Comment text is written by the public and can occasionally contain names, handles or other personal details. We don't search for these or use them to identify anyone.

3. Why we collect it

To measure, across many comments at once, which topics come up, what tone people use, and how that relates to the wider audience conversation around the client's posts. Results are reported in aggregate (for example, "a large share of comments on posts about topic X were critical"). Any comment shown as an example in a report has names and handles removed.

We do not:

  • build profiles of individual commenters, or label or guess any individual's beliefs, politics, religion or identity;
  • use the data for advertising, marketing, or to contact anyone;
  • sell, rent or trade the data;
  • make any decision about an individual person from this data.

4. How we protect commenters' identities

  • When comments are saved, each username is replaced with a one-way, salted code (for example u_3fa91c07de). Our analysis files contain these codes, not the usernames.
  • The secret "salt" used to make the codes is kept separately, so the codes cannot be reversed by anyone who only has the analysis files.
  • We can still honour your deletion request (see below): if you tell us your username, we can generate your code and delete your comments.

5. Who sees the data, and where it goes

  • The client receives aggregated findings. They already own the posts and can see the comments on their own account.
  • Storage. Data is stored on an encrypted drive on the consultant's computer in India. Only Gokulakrishna Ramachandran has access.
  • We don't share the data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

Raw comment data is deleted 90 days after the final report goes to the client, or earlier if the client asks. Fully aggregated results (counts and percentages that cannot be traced to a person) may be kept longer. The Instagram access token is deleted when the project ends, and the account owner can cut it off at any moment (see section 7).

7. Your choices and rights

  • Account owner: you can switch access off yourself, at any time, on Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → Active → remove this app (the exact wording varies by Instagram version). That cancels our token immediately.
  • Anyone who commented: you can ask us what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. See Data deletion.
  • We try to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and we're happy to deal with requests from people anywhere.

8. Security

The access token is kept in a file restricted to our own user account and is never put in code or shared. Data sits on a device protected by full-disk encryption and a password. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to keep this small, simple, and locked down. If we ever discover a breach affecting you, we will tell the client and, where appropriate, you.

9. Children

This tool isn't aimed at children and we don't knowingly collect data about anyone under 18 beyond what appears in public comments. Instagram has its own age rules.

10. Meta and Instagram

This tool uses Meta's Instagram API and follows Meta's Platform Terms. It is not made, endorsed or sponsored by Meta or Instagram.

Data deletion instructions

If you commented on a post and want your data deleted

  1. Email gookkuul@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my data".
  2. Tell us your Instagram username (we need it to work out your code, because we don't store usernames in readable form). A link to a post you commented on helps, but isn't required.
  3. We may ask you to confirm you control that Instagram account, for example by messaging us from it, so nobody can delete someone else's data by mistake.
  4. We delete every comment linked to your code from our files and confirm by email within 30 days.

If you are the Instagram account owner

  1. Remove access yourself on Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → Active → remove this app. This stops all further access straight away.
  2. Email gookkuul@gmail.com with the subject "Delete account data" from the email linked to the account (or tell us the username). We delete the posts, comments, token and analysis files for your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deletion removes the data from our working files. It can't recall anything the client already received in an aggregated report, because that report doesn't contain data traceable to you.
